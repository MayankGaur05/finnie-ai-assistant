# Income Tax in India: A Basic Overview

## Summary

Income tax is a direct tax calculated under tax law on income attributed to a person or entity for a relevant tax period. Tax collected by the government helps fund public services. Whether tax is payable, whether a return must be filed, and how income is calculated depend on the applicable law and a taxpayer's circumstances.

Income may come from several sources, including:

- **Salary or wages** from employment.
- **Business or professional activity**, including self-employment.
- **Capital gains** from transferring assets, where the law treats the gain as taxable.
- **House property**, which may include rental income.
- **Other sources**, which can include items such as interest or dividends, depending on the rules.

When determining tax obligations, relevant details can include the taxpayer's residence status, income sources, deductions or exemptions available, chosen tax regime where applicable, and the tax period. Some people or entities may have filing obligations even where their final tax payable is nil; the applicable rules decide.

## India's tax-period transition

The Income-tax Act, 2025 took effect on April 1, 2026. It applies to tax years beginning on or after that date. Income and proceedings relating to relevant earlier tax years remain subject to the Income-tax Act, 1961 and transitional provisions. Use the law and forms for the particular tax period; do not assume the terminology or filing steps in an older article are current.

## Limitation

This article is for general education and does not calculate tax, identify a required return form, or provide filing advice. The ClearTax source page was updated in February 2025 and predates the 2026 transition. Tax rates, thresholds, deductions, return requirements, and deadlines can change. Check the Income Tax Department's current guidance for the relevant period or consult a qualified tax professional.
