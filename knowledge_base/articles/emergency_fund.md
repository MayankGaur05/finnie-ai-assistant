# Emergency Fund

## Summary

An emergency fund is money set aside for unplanned expenses or financial emergencies. Examples can include car or home repairs, medical bills, and a loss of income.

- Savings for unexpected costs may help people manage a financial shock without missing other bills or relying on borrowing.
- The amount to save depends on a person's circumstances. Looking at unexpected expenses they have faced before can help them choose a savings goal.
- A dedicated place for emergency savings can make the money easier to identify and access when needed. The CFPB suggests considering safety, accessibility, and the risk of spending it on non-emergencies when choosing where to keep it.
- People can set guidelines for what counts as an emergency. If they use some of the fund, they can work toward rebuilding it over time.

## Limitation

This article provides general financial education, not individualized financial, tax, or investment advice. It does not prescribe a specific savings target or account; appropriate choices depend on a person's situation.
